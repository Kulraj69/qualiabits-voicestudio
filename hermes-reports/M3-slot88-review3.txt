The evidence material is overly long and defensive, but the underlying UI and copy are designed rather than dumped.

The writing is specific to this repository: it names routes, engines, failure modes, measurements, and unresolved work. It avoids the prohibited generic startup language and plainly says “Not deployed,” “No GPU,” and “No human has listened to the audio.” The repeated em-dash construction and evidence-heavy prose are noticeable, but not enough to make it AI-generic.

4. Technical depth — 4/5

The slice contains real browser geometry checks across nine routes and three viewport sizes, per-element overflow inspection, screenshot capture, a corrected top-bar row counter, stylesheet-preservation guards, and regression tests. The wider hosted product also has authentication, metering, limits, jobs, transcription, dubbing, projects, and real-engine evidence, backed by the stated 270 hosted tests, 118 web unit tests, and 165-check real-engine run. This is solid engineering, although the slice itself is primarily responsive CSS and evidence hardening rather than a difficult new product capability.

5. Criteria fit — 2/5

The slice fits the narrow M3 responsive-dashboard objective: nine pages, responsive behavior, dark/light support, screenshots, browser checks, and documented acceptance rows. It does not evidence major requirements of the owner’s V0 milestone: Voice Clone, Voice Design, voice agents, phone calls, public deployment, HTTPS, real S3, GPU autoscaling, public signup, M1 GPU/listening evidence, the demo video, or M7 growth work. These omissions are explicitly acknowledged in `hosted/M3-ACCEPTANCE.md:98-100, 248-249` and `hosted/web/README.md:16-18`.

The cited runs, acceptance matrix, pinned model checks, and cold-clone transcript make the claims unusually auditable. 7. Honesty — 4/5

The artifacts consistently distinguish stub from real-engine evidence and state that the product is not deployed, has no public URL, uses no real S3 or GPU, and has not had its audio human-checked. The earlier contradictory cold-clone count and unsupported “pixel-for-pixel” desktop claim were corrected with guards and narrower wording. The `.table-wrap` overflow exception is now disclosed in both `hosted/M3-ACCEPTANCE.md` and `hosted/web/README.md`; no direct honesty flaw remains in the supplied final artifacts.
