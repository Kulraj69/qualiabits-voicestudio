Scores

2. Taste: 3/5

3. Human feel: 3/5  
The writing is specific and candid in places, especially around unbuilt cloning/design and unreviewed audio. The supplied evidence establishes the accessibility slice, not comprehensive M1–M3 bug closure. Voice cloning and voice design remain unbuilt, and deployment is absent as acknowledged in `hosted/M4-ACCEPTANCE.md`.

6. Reviewer path: 4/5

The evidence does not support the D7 claim that the cold clone was green at the same pushed revision as the browser paths. The real and stub browser runs cite commit `21d7de111` (`M4-slot104-web-e2e-r1/result.json` and the stub result), while `V0-slot104-coldclone-r1.log` says the cold clone was at `5c9542217`. Five most damaging flaws

1. Cold-clone revision mismatch — fatal  
   `M4-slot104-web-e2e-r1/result.json`: tested commit `21d7de111`; `V0-slot104-coldclone-r1.log`: clone revision `5c954221769e...`; commit list later reaches `45af954bb`. D7 requires the cold-clone evidence at the pushed revision, but these artifacts are not at one revision.

4. Accessibility is inferred, not tested with an actual screen reader  
   `hosted/web/e2e/dashboard.mjs:793-825, 950-1000`: the suite runs axe and simulates Tab traversal, but no NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack, or other assistive-technology transcript is supplied. Axe and keyboard reachability are strong proxies, not proof of the complete screen-reader experience.

5. The real-engine transcript evidence exposes product-quality errors  
   `M4-slot104-web-e2e-r1/result.json`, around the transcription checks at `2026-11-07T22:18:07Z`: spoken Hindi is recorded as `यह एक हिन्दी वाक्य है।`, while the engine returns `यह एक हिंदी वाग के है`. What would make this win: retake both browser paths and the complete cold-clone verification from one final pushed commit, then present a shorter, human-readable demo showing a real assistive-technology workflow and evidence that the broader M4 bug-fix/test milestone—not only this accessibility slice—is complete.
