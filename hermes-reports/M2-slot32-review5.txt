Five most damaging flaws

1. Evidence revision mismatch — `hosted/README.md:161–163` claims the clean-clone log is from the same revision as E2E commit `e875b116b`; Artifact 6 says its clone is `05ba314...` at `2026-11-01T08:25:07+05:30`.
2. Fresh-environment claim contradicted — `hosted/README.md:161–162` says the venv was built by the README commands; Artifact 6 says “venv reused from the working copy.”
3. AI attribution in product source — `hosted/control_plane/storage.py:22` says “Written by Hermes for QualiaBits,” undermining the required human/authentic presentation.
4. The core product is not actually deployed — Artifact 4 records `deployed: false`, `public_url: null`, and `listening_verified: false`; this is acceptable for the stated milestone, but prevents any claim of a hosted customer-ready service.
5. S3 is not production-verified — `hosted/control_plane/storage.py:135–143` explicitly says the S3 backend has never run against a real bucket. Emulator and stub tests prove client logic, not credentials, IAM, lifecycle, region, or real-bucket behavior.

What would make it win: remove the evidence contradictions, rerun the clean-clone tests and E2E on one immutable revision in a genuinely fresh environment, and present one concise reviewer path with a real hosted demo or verifiable deployment.
